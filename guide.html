User Guide

Everything you need to know to get started with CrownPhone Box. From unboxing to advanced features, we've got you covered.

Getting Started Setup Features Support

Getting Started

1

Unbox Your Device

Carefully unbox your CrownPhone device. Inside, you'll find the main unit, power adapter, USB cables, and quick start guide. Make sure all components are present before proceeding.

2

Connect Power

Connect the power adapter to your device and plug it into a power outlet. The LED indicator should light up, showing that the device is receiving power.

3

Connect to Display

Use the included HDMI cable to connect your CrownPhone Box to your monitor or TV. Select the correct HDMI input on your display.

Pro Tip

For the best experience, use a 4K compatible HDMI cable if your display supports 4K resolution. This ensures you get the maximum quality from your CrownPhone Box.

Initial Setup

1

Install Software

Download the CrownPhone software from your email confirmation or from your profile dashboard. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.

2

Enter License Key

Launch the application and enter your license key when prompted. This key was sent to your email after purchase. Keep it safe for future use.

3

Configure Settings

Adjust video quality, audio settings, and connection preferences according to your needs. The software will guide you through the initial configuration.

Important

Make sure your device and computer are connected to the same Wi-Fi network for wireless mirroring. For wired connections, use a USB-C cable with data transfer support.

Key Features

Screen Mirroring

Mirror your phone, tablet, or computer screen in real-time with ultra-low latency. Perfect for presentations, gaming, and content sharing.

4K Resolution Support

Enjoy crystal-clear 4K resolution output for the best visual experience. Automatically adjusts to your display's capabilities.

Wireless & Wired

Connect wirelessly via Wi-Fi or use a USB-C cable for a stable wired connection. Both options offer excellent performance.

Screen Recording

Record your screen sessions with high quality. Perfect for creating tutorials, capturing gameplay, or saving presentations.

Multi-Device Support

Connect multiple devices simultaneously (plan-dependent). Switch between devices seamlessly during your session.

Secure Connection

All connections are encrypted end-to-end. Your data remains private and secure during transmission.
