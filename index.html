Everything you need to know about Crown Screens

How does Crown Screens work?

Crown Screens uses advanced device management technology to create a centralized control system for multiple devices. Simply connect your devices via USB or WiFi, and you can monitor and control up to 100 devices simultaneously from a single dashboard.

Instant connection
HD quality output
Secure & private

What devices are supported?

Crown Screens supports all Android devices running Android 5.0 and above. For PC, we support Windows 10/11, macOS 10.14+, and Linux distributions. The software automatically detects your devices and optimizes settings for the best performance.

Android 5.0+
Windows 10/11
macOS 10.14+
